Our Story

From a small office to a growing ecosystem.

Creative Code Hub opened its doors in October 2024 in Kakinada with a simple idea: college graduates deserve better preparation for the jobs they actually want. The gap between an academic degree and an industry offer was too wide — and too many students were falling into it.

In just the first year, we built the missing bridge. Structured courses, real projects, industry mentors, internships and placement support — combined into six specialized hubs that support students at every stage.

We're still young. But already working with colleges across Andhra Pradesh, training hundreds of students, and helping interns step into real roles.

What drives us

Vision, mission, values, approach.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted learning and career platform in South India — where any student, from any background, can build the skills and access the opportunities to launch a strong career.

Our Mission

To deliver industry-aligned education, real projects and genuine career support — so students don't just graduate, they get hired.

Our Values

Learning first. Craftsmanship in every course. Genuine care for each student. Ethical practice, always. Continuous improvement.

Our Approach

Practical, mentor-led, project-based. Every program ends with something you can show a recruiter.
